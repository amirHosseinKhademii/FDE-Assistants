1. SCOPE

   This specification covers the electric power steering system for the
   Gorse programme (C-EPS, B segment).
   Start of production 2029-01-24.

2. DEFINITIONS

   shall   mandatory. Non-compliance requires a written concession.
   should  recommended. Deviation requires justification at the design review.
   must    mandatory.

   

3. CHANGE RECORD

   Rev A   2021-04-09   Initial issue for quotation.

   Changed requirements are marked with a bar in the left margin.

4. REQUIREMENTS

  1. [CR-CHV-07-0115] road wheel angle deg. The system shall achieve 48.86 deg. (test, ASIL QM)

  2. [CR-CHV-07-0116] rack force capacity. The system shall achieve 7.6kN. (inspection, ASIL C)

  3. rack force capacity. The system should achieve 10701 N. (review, ASIL QM)

  4. assist latency ms. The system shall achieve 6.7 ms. (review, ASIL B)

  5. road wheel angle deg. The system shall achieve 53.7 deg. (test, ASIL QM)

  6. [CR-CHV-07-0120] road wheel angle deg. The system shall achieve 45 deg. (analysis, ASIL B)

  7. [CR-CHV-07-0121] on center hysteresis nm. The system shall achieve 0.85 Nm. (test, ASIL C)

  8. mass kg. The system shall achieve 12.92 kg. (inspection, ASIL QM)

  9. [CR-CHV-07-0123] max current a. The system shall achieve 113 A. (analysis, ASIL C)

  10. [CR-CHV-07-0124] nvh limit db. The system shall achieve 48.69 dB. (review, ASIL D)

  11. [CR-CHV-07-0125] road wheel angle deg. The system shall achieve 55.62 deg. (test, ASIL D)

  12. max current a. The system shall achieve 75 A. (inspection, ASIL QM)

  13. [CR-CHV-07-0127] rack force capacity. The system should achieve 6125 N. (review, ASIL QM)

  14. [CR-CHV-07-0128] road wheel angle deg. The system must achieve 53.4 deg. (test, ASIL B)



5. VERIFICATION

   Verification method per requirement is given above. Where the method is
   "test", the supplier must provide a test report referencing this
   document and the requirement identifier.
