## About This Endorsement

This endorsement amends PP 00 01 06 24 for risks located in New York. **Where
this endorsement is silent, PP 00 01 06 24 controls.** It applies only to
policies issued in New York; it has no effect on a policy issued elsewhere.

## Part IV — Physical Damage Coverage

### 4.3 Schedule of Limits and Deductibles

The following limits replace those shown in PP 00 01 06 24 for risks in
New York.

| Coverage | Limit | Deductible |
|---|---|---|
| Collision | Actual cash value | $1,000 |
| Comprehensive | Actual cash value | $500 |
| Rental Reimbursement | $40 per day, 30 days max | None |
| Bodily Injury Liability | $25,000 / $50,000 | None |
| Property Damage Liability | $10,000 | None |

## Part V — New York Amendments

### 5.1 New York Amendments

**Personal Injury Protection** is mandatory and applies at $50,000 per person
regardless of the amount shown in the Schedule above.

**Supplementary Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist Coverage** is available at the
Bodily Injury limits and applies unless rejected in writing.

**Glass.** Full glass repair is available without application of the
Comprehensive deductible where the glass is repaired rather than replaced.

## All Other Provisions

All other provisions of PP 00 01 06 24 apply unchanged.
