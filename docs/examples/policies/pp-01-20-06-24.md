## About This Endorsement

This endorsement amends PP 00 01 06 24 for risks located in Florida. **Where
this endorsement is silent, PP 00 01 06 24 controls.** It applies only to
policies issued in Florida; it has no effect on a policy issued elsewhere.

## Part V — Florida Amendments

### 5.1 Florida Amendments

**Personal Injury Protection** applies at $10,000 and is primary for medical
expenses arising from a covered accident, subject to the 14-day initial
treatment requirement.

**Named storms.** Hurricane and named-storm losses are Comprehensive losses and
are subject to the Comprehensive deductible shown above, not to a separate
percentage deductible.

**Assignment of benefits** to a repair facility requires our written consent.
