# POL-GDW-003 Rev 2 — Goodwill gestures and approval thresholds

> Revision Id: POL-GDW-003 Rev 2 · Doc Id: POL-GDW-003 · Revision: 2 · Status: current · Effective: 2025-01-13 · Expires: · Owner: DEPT-CARE · Category: policy · Audience: internal · Supersedes: POL-GDW-003 Rev 1

*FABRICATED. Thornbury Goods does not exist and this policy was never applied.
See [CORPUS.md](../CORPUS.md).*

## 1. Purpose

Sets what may be offered as a gesture where the customer is not strictly
entitled to anything, and who must approve money leaving the business.

## 2. What a goodwill gesture is

A payment or credit made where the evidence does **not** establish an
entitlement, but the customer's experience warrants recognition.

**A goodwill gesture is not a small refund.** A refund says we accept the claim;
a gesture says we do not, and are paying anyway. Recording one as the other
destroys the only number that tells us how often we are actually at fault.

## 3. What may be offered

| Situation | Ceiling |
|---|---|
| Delivery late against the applicable contract | £10 or 10% of order value, whichever is greater |
| Delivery experience poor but on time | £10 |
| Damage claimed, evidence silent, item value under £50 | full item value |
| Damage claimed, evidence silent, item value £50 or over | escalate — no ceiling applies without approval |
| Repeat contact on the same order | £15 in addition to any other outcome |

A gesture is offered once per order. A second gesture on the same order is an
escalation regardless of amount.

## 4. Approval thresholds — who may release money

| Amount | Approved by |
|---|---|
| Up to £50 | the specialist handling the case |
| £50.01 – £250 | a team leader |
| £250.01 – £1,000 | the care manager |
| Over £1,000 | care manager and a commercial signatory |

Three rules that override the table:

**4.1** Any outcome on an order line that has **already had a refund** requires a
team leader, whatever the amount. A duplicate refund is the failure this rule
exists to prevent and it is worth the friction.

**4.2** Any outcome resolving a **recorded conflict** requires a team leader,
whatever the amount. If two sources disagree and someone has decided which is
right, that decision has an owner.

**4.3** **Approval is a person, not a status.** The approving individual is named
on the case. "System approved", "auto-approved" and an unattributed tick are not
approvals, and a resolution carrying one is not complete.

## 5. What may never be offered

- A gesture in exchange for withdrawing a complaint or removing a review.
- A gesture conditional on the customer not contacting us again.
- Any amount agreed verbally and not recorded on the case before it is paid.

## 6. Recording

Every gesture records the amount, the reason, the approving person, and the
evidence that was **not** sufficient. That last field is the one that gets left
blank and the only one that tells us anything: a rising count of gestures paid
for the same missing evidence is a defect in a process, not a run of generous
decisions.
