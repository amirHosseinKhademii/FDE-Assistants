Schedule 2 of the carriage agreement between Thornbury Goods Ltd and Pelham
Carriers Ltd. Extracted for the resolutions desk.

## 1. Services

| Service | Code | Commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | PLM-STD | delivered within 3 **calendar days** of collection |
| Express | PLM-EXP | delivered within 1 **calendar day** of collection |

## 2. Calendar days — and the trap of assuming both carriers count alike

**Pelham's commitments are in CALENDAR days. Every day counts, including
weekends and bank holidays.**

This is not the same definition Northgate uses, and the difference is the single
most common source of a wrong lateness answer on this desk. **The same parcel,
collected on the same Friday, is due on different days depending on which
carrier took it:**

```
  collected Friday, Christmas the following Monday

  Northgate NGT-ND   next WORKING day    →  due Wednesday
  Pelham    PLM-EXP  next CALENDAR day   →  due Saturday
```

**Determine the carrier before computing lateness.** The answer is not a
property of the order; it is a property of the contract the parcel travelled
under.

## 3. Failure and remedy

Where Pelham misses a commitment, 50% of the carriage charge is credited. There
is no monthly service credit in this agreement.

As with Northgate, this is a commercial remedy between Thornbury and Pelham and
is not the customer's entitlement.

## 4. Damage in transit

Pelham accepts liability for damage between collection and delivery, subject to
a claim within **14 days** of delivery — half Northgate's window.

Again: this is our window against Pelham, not the customer's window against us.

## 5. Proof of delivery

**Pelham does not capture delivery photographs.** They capture a signature where
someone is present and a GPS point where the parcel was left.

**The absence of a photograph on a Pelham delivery means Pelham does not take
photographs.** It is not evidence about that delivery, and it must never be
treated as a gap in the customer's case.
