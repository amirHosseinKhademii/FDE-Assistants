# BUL-HV-2025-01 — High-value items: additional verification before refund

> Revision Id: BUL-HV-2025-01 · Doc Id: BUL-HV-2025-01 · Revision: 1 · Status: current · Effective: 2025-01-20 · Expires: · Owner: DEPT-CARE · Category: bulletin · Audience: internal · Amends: POL-DOA-002

*FABRICATED. Thornbury Goods does not exist and this bulletin was never issued.
See [CORPUS.md](../CORPUS.md).*

## 1. What changes

For any single item with a sale price of **£400 or more**, a damage or
non-delivery claim requires two further checks before an outcome is proposed:

1. **The item must be seen** — returned, collected, or photographed by the
   customer showing the item and its serial or batch marking together.
2. **The delivery address must match** an address used on a previous completed
   order for this account, or the account must be older than 90 days.

Where either check cannot be satisfied, escalate. Do not refuse on this basis
alone.

## 2. Why

Claim value above £400 grew 3.4× in 2024 against order volume growth of 1.2×.
The pattern sits in a narrow band of first-order, new-address, high-value
claims.

## 3. What this bulletin is careful not to say

**It does not say that a high-value claim is likely to be fraudulent**, and it
must not be read that way or quoted that way to a customer. The overwhelming
majority of high-value claims are exactly what they appear to be, and a customer
whose £600 item genuinely arrived smashed is entitled to the same outcome as one
whose £30 item did — arriving at it takes two more steps.

**Nothing in this bulletin changes an entitlement.** It changes the evidence
required to establish one. A claim that satisfies §1 is decided under
POL-DOA-002 exactly as any other claim would be.

## 4. Interaction with approval thresholds

An item at £400 or above will usually also cross the team leader threshold in
POL-GDW-003 §4. **These are two separate requirements and satisfying one does
not satisfy the other.** Verification is about the evidence; approval is about
who releases the money.
