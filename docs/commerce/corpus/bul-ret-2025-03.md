# BUL-RET-2025-03 — Returns window for electronics: reverting to 14 days

> Revision Id: BUL-RET-2025-03 · Doc Id: BUL-RET-2025-03 · Revision: 1 · Status: current · Effective: 2025-03-01 · Expires: · Owner: DEPT-CARE · Category: bulletin · Audience: internal · Amends: POL-RET-001

*FABRICATED. Thornbury Goods does not exist and this bulletin was never issued.
See [CORPUS.md](../CORPUS.md).*

## 1. What changes

**From 2025-03-01 the returns window for items in the `electronics` category is
14 days from delivery, not the 30 days stated in POL-RET-001 Rev 3 §2.**

Every other category is unchanged at 30 days.

## 2. Why

Change-of-mind returns of electronics ran at 4.1× the rate of other categories
in the 2024 peak, and a disproportionate share came back after 21 days with
evidence of sustained use. The 30-day window in Rev 3 was not written with that
pattern in view.

## 3. Where this has been applied

The returns tool reads its window from the `return_windows` configuration table,
and **that table was updated on 2025-03-01 to carry 14 days for electronics.**
The tool therefore already behaves as this bulletin describes.

## 4. Where this has NOT been applied

**POL-RET-001 Rev 3 has not been reissued.** The public returns page still says
30 days for every category, and a customer reading it today is being told 30.

This was raised at the time and deferred: reissuing the public policy was judged
to need legal review that would not complete before peak, and the bulletin was
treated as sufficient in the interim. The interim has not ended.

**Consequence for the resolutions desk.** For an electronics item returned
between day 15 and day 30, the configuration says refuse and the published
policy says accept. **Neither is wrong and this bulletin does not settle it.**
A customer who read the public page and relied on it has an argument that does
not depend on what our configuration says.

**Escalate these rather than deciding them.** The right answer depends on what
the customer was shown and when, which is not a fact this document contains.

## 5. Category assignment is not defined here

This bulletin says what happens to items in the `electronics` category. It does
not say which products are in it. Category is assigned in the product record,
and there is no published mapping from a product to its category.

Items with both a decorative and an electronic character — lighting with
integrated controls, powered furniture, connected kitchenware — have been
assigned inconsistently. **Where category is genuinely arguable, the category on
the product record is a fact about our database and not a fact about the item.**
