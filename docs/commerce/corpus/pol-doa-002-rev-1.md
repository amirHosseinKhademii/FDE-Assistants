# POL-DOA-002 Rev 1 — Damaged on Arrival: evidence and decision

> Revision Id: POL-DOA-002 Rev 1 · Doc Id: POL-DOA-002 · Revision: 1 · Status: current · Effective: 2025-02-03 · Expires: · Owner: DEPT-CARE · Category: procedure · Audience: internal

*FABRICATED. Thornbury Goods does not exist and this procedure was never
followed. See [CORPUS.md](../CORPUS.md).*

## 1. Purpose

A customer says their order arrived damaged. This procedure defines what
evidence to gather, how to weigh it, and who decides.

## 2. Scope

Applies where the customer states the item was damaged when it reached them. It
does **not** apply to change of mind (POL-RET-001), to a fault that developed in
use, or to an item that never arrived.

## 3. The window

A damage claim should be raised within 30 days of delivery. **A claim raised
later is not thereby refused** — damage concealed by packaging can go unnoticed
for weeks, and the statutory position in REF-LAW-UK-2024 §3 does not expire when
our window does. A late claim is weighed, not dismissed.

## 4. The evidence, and the order to gather it

Gather all five before deciding. Stopping at the first that answers the question
is how the wrong decisions get made.

**4.1 The order record.** What was bought, what it cost, and — before anything
else — **whether any refund has already been issued against this order line.**
A second refund on an already-refunded line is the most expensive error on this
desk and the cheapest to prevent.

**4.2 The delivery record.** The carrier's scans, the delivery event and its
exception code, and the proof of delivery where one exists.

**4.3 The proof-of-delivery photograph.** Where the carrier captured one, look
at it. A photograph of an intact parcel on a doorstep is evidence about the
parcel's condition at that doorstep and about nothing else.

**4.4 The route record — and this is the step that gets skipped.**

> A delivery event with no exception code means **the driver did not record
> one**. It does not mean nothing happened.
>
> An incident that affects several parcels is logged **against the route**,
> under STD-DEP-004, not against each parcel it touched. So a parcel damaged in
> a trolley tip, a load shift, or a dropped cage will have a clean delivery
> event and a route with an incident on it.
>
> **Read the driver's reports for the route on the delivery date, and find the
> stop this order was delivered at.** A shipment row alone cannot tell you this
> and will look reassuring while doing so.

**4.5 The customer's own account**, and any photographs they supplied.

## 5. Weighing it

Where the evidence agrees, decide.

**Where the customer's account and the carrier's record disagree, that is a
conflict and it is recorded as one.** It is not resolved by preferring the
record. A clean delivery event is weak evidence of an undamaged parcel — it
records what a driver entered on a handset at the door, under time pressure,
about a box they did not open.

**A conflict is escalated with both positions stated and their sources named.**
Do not choose between them on this desk.

## 6. Outcomes

| | when |
|---|---|
| Full refund | damage is established, or the evidence is silent and the value is below the goodwill threshold in POL-GDW-003 |
| Replacement | the customer prefers it and stock exists |
| Collection and refund | the item is bulky, hazardous, or must be seen |
| Partial refund | the customer wishes to keep a damaged item at a reduced price, agreed in writing |
| Goodwill only | damage is not established but the customer's experience warrants a gesture. POL-GDW-003 governs the amount |
| Escalate | a conflict under §5, a prior refund under §4.1, a value above the approval threshold, or any claim where the evidence does not support a decision |

## 7. Who bears the cost

Whether the carrier is recharged is a commercial matter settled between
Thornbury and the carrier under the relevant contract. **It has no bearing on
what the customer is owed and must never delay the customer's outcome.**

## 8. What this procedure does not decide

It does not decide whether money moves. **Every outcome in §6 that pays a
customer is a proposal until a named person approves it**, and the approval
thresholds are in POL-GDW-003 §4.
