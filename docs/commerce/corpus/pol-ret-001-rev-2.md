## 1. Purpose

States what a customer may return, by when, and what they get back.

## 2. The standard window

**A customer may return most items within 28 days of delivery.**

## 3. Category windows

The 28-day window in section 2 does not apply uniformly. The following
categories carry their own windows:

| Category | Window | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Homeware | 28 days | the standard window |
| Apparel | 28 days | unworn, tags attached |
| Electronics | 14 days | sealed items only; see the note below |
| Perishables | not returnable | except where faulty |

**Electronics.** An electronic item may be returned within 14 days if it has not
been unsealed. Once unsealed it may be returned only if faulty.

## 4. Condition

Items must be returned complete and in a resaleable condition.

## 5. Delivery charges

Outbound delivery is refunded only where the item was faulty or misdescribed.

## 6. Why this revision was replaced

Rev 3 removed the category table in section 3 and applied a single 30-day window
to everything. The stated reason at the time was that the table was generating
more disputes than it prevented, because customers did not agree with our
category assignments and there was no published list of which products sat in
which category.
