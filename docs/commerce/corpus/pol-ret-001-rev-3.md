# POL-RET-001 Rev 3 — Returns and Refunds Policy

## 1. Purpose

This is the policy Thornbury publishes to customers. It states what a customer
may return, by when, and what they get back. It is written to be quoted at us
and should be read as a promise rather than as guidance.

## 2. The standard window

**A customer may return any item within 30 days of delivery**, for any reason or
no reason, and receive a full refund of the item price.

Thirty days runs from the delivery date recorded against the order, not from the
despatch date and not from the order date. Where no delivery date was recorded,
the window runs from the last carrier scan.

This window applies to **every category we sell**. Rev 2 of this policy carried
a shorter window for some categories; that distinction was removed in this
revision to make the promise simple to state and simple to honour.

## 3. Condition of the returned item

The item must be returned complete, with any accessories and any free gift that
came with it. It may have been unpacked and inspected. A customer is entitled to
handle goods as they would in a shop.

We may reduce a refund where handling has gone beyond inspection and reduced the
value of the item. A reduction must be justified in writing on the case and must
never be applied to an item returned because it arrived damaged or faulty.

## 4. Delivery charges

The original outbound delivery charge is refunded with the item where the return
is because the item was damaged, faulty, or not what was ordered.

Where the return is a change of mind, the outbound charge is retained and the
customer bears the cost of return carriage.

## 5. Items that arrived damaged or faulty

This policy sets the window. **The evidence required, and the decision about who
bears the cost, are in POL-DOA-002** — a damaged-on-arrival claim is not a
change-of-mind return with a different label, and treating it as one is the most
common error on the resolutions desk.

A damaged or faulty item is not subject to the 30-day window in section 2 for
the purposes of the customer's statutory rights. See REF-LAW-UK-2024.

## 6. Refund method and timing

A refund is issued to the original payment method. Where that method is no
longer valid, a credit to the customer's account is offered and the customer's
agreement is recorded on the case.

A refund is issued within 14 days of us receiving the item back, or within 14
days of the customer providing evidence of return, whichever is earlier.

## 7. What this policy does not cover

This policy covers goods sold by Thornbury Goods. It does not address gift
cards, digital content, or services.

## 8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy are issued as bulletins and are binding from their
effective date. **A bulletin narrows or widens this policy without this document
being reissued**, which means this page can be current and incomplete at the
same time. Check for bulletins before quoting section 2 as final.
