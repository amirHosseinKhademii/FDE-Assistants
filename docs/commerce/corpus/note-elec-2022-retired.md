# NOTE-ELEC-2022 — Guidance: electronics returns (RETIRED)

> Revision Id: NOTE-ELEC-2022 · Doc Id: NOTE-ELEC-2022 · Revision: 1 · Status: retired · Effective: 2022-02-14 · Expires: 2023-05-31 · Owner: DEPT-CARE · Category: guidance · Audience: internal

*FABRICATED. Thornbury Goods does not exist and this note was never circulated.
See [CORPUS.md](../CORPUS.md).*

> **RETIRED. This note is wrong and has been wrong since 2023-06-01.** It is kept
> only because it is quoted in old case notes and somebody reading those needs to
> be able to find out that what they are quoting was withdrawn.

## 1. Position

**An electronic item that has been unsealed may not be returned.** Once the seal
on the box is broken the item is no longer resaleable as new and the sale is
final, except where the item is faulty.

## 2. Handling the conversation

Where a customer asks to return an unsealed electronic item that is not faulty,
decline and explain that the seal is the condition of the return.

## 3. Why this was withdrawn

This note misstated the law. A customer's right to reject goods bought at
distance does not depend on packaging being intact — a customer is entitled to
handle goods as they would in a shop, and unsealing a box to look at what is
inside is inspection, not use.

Applying this note cost Thornbury a run of complaints in 2022–23 and at least
one referral. It was withdrawn on 2023-06-01 when POL-RET-001 Rev 2 took effect.

**Anything in this note that conflicts with POL-RET-001 or REF-LAW-UK-2024 is
wrong, and POL-RET-001 and REF-LAW-UK-2024 are right.**
