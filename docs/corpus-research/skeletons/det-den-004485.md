## What You Claimed

You claimed rental expense incurred between 2024-09-19 and 2024-09-27 while your
covered auto was being repaired following a covered collision.

## What Our Investigation Found

The collision loss itself is covered and has been paid. Our review of the
declarations page in force on the date of loss found that Rental Reimbursement
is not shown with a premium charge on this policy.

## The Policy Provisions We Relied On

| Provision | Document | Text relied on, in substance |
|---|---|---|
| Essential Line Restrictions | PE-2023-01 5.1 | Rental Reimbursement is optional on this form and applies only where shown on the Declarations with a premium charge |
| Declarations | AUT-4485 | No premium charge shown for Rental Reimbursement |

## How Those Provisions Apply To These Facts

Section 5.1 of PE-2023-01 makes the rental benefit optional and conditions it on
the coverage appearing on the Declarations with a premium charge. The
Declarations for this policy shows no such charge. The form itself states that
the absence of a premium charge means the benefit was not purchased. The rental
expense you incurred is therefore not payable under this policy.
