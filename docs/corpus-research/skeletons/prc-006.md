# PRC-006 — Claims Handling Procedures: Physical Damage and Loss of Use

> Meridian Mutual Insurance Company. Fictional document, written for an FDE
> practice engagement. Do not use for anything real.

> Document: PRC-006 · Revision 4 · Effective: 2023-02-01 · Owner: Claims Practices

## 0. About This Document

This document sets conduct standards for handling the physical damage and loss
of use portions of a personal auto claim. It tells an adjuster how to act. It
does not determine coverage. Coverage is determined by the policy form, the
endorsements attached to it, and the declarations page for the individual
policyholder. Where this document and a policy form appear to disagree about
what is payable, the form governs and the disagreement is a defect in this
document to be reported to Claims Practices.

Bulletins issued after the revision date of this document prevail over the
section they name. Current bulletins are published in the claims reference
library. An adjuster who applies a section of this document that a later
bulletin has replaced has not followed procedure.

## 1. Intake and Acknowledgement

### 1.1 First Notice of Loss

The first notice of loss is recorded on receipt regardless of the channel it
arrives on. A notice taken by telephone is entered the same business day. A
notice arriving outside business hours is entered on the next business day and
the acknowledgement clock runs from the date of receipt, not the date of entry.

| Event | Standard | Measured from | Exception |
|---|---|---|---|
| Record notice | Same business day | Receipt | None |
| Acknowledge claimant | 2 business days | Receipt | Catastrophe bulletin in force |
| Assign adjuster | 2 business days | Receipt | Catastrophe bulletin in force |
| First substantive contact | 5 business days | Assignment | Claimant unreachable, documented |

### 1.2 Acknowledging the Claimant

The acknowledgement identifies the claim number, the assigned adjuster, and the
coverages under which the claim is being handled. Where more than one coverage
may respond, each is named. Failing to name a coverage that may respond is a
misrepresentation of policy provisions and is examined as such.

### 1.3 Assigning the File

| Claim characteristic | Assign to | Referral required |
|---|---|---|
| Repairable, single vehicle | Adjuster | No |
| Suspected total loss | Adjuster with appraisal unit | No |
| Coverage question open | Senior adjuster | Yes |
| Endorsement status unconfirmed | Senior adjuster | Yes |
| Prior determination on same policy | Senior adjuster | Yes |

## 2. Establishing the Governing Documents

Before any coverage position is taken, the file records which documents govern.
This is a documentation step, not a judgment step, and it is done in this order.

1. Retrieve the declarations in force on the date of loss.
2. Record the form and edition named on it.
3. Record every endorsement listed on its schedule of forms.
4. Record the rated state.

An endorsement a claimant asserts but which does not appear on the schedule of
forms in force on the date of loss is not recorded as governing. The assertion
is recorded, and the file is referred.

| Field | Source | Recorded as |
|---|---|---|
| Form and edition | Declarations | Governing form |
| Endorsements | Declarations schedule of forms | Governing amendments |
| Rated state | Declarations | Jurisdiction |
| Date of loss | First notice | Effective-date test |

## 3. Reserves

| Claim type | Initial reserve basis | Review trigger | Re-reserve interval |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repairable | Appraisal estimate | Supplement received | 30 days |
| Suspected total loss | Preliminary valuation | Valuation report | 15 days |
| Loss of use | Daily rate times expected days | Repair delay | 15 days |
| Coverage in question | Nominal, pending position | Position taken | 15 days |

A reserve is not a coverage position and is not communicated to a claimant as
one.

## 4. Investigation

### 4.1 Contact Standards

Contact is attempted by two channels before a claimant is recorded as
unreachable, and each attempt is noted with date, time and channel. Three
unreturned attempts across two channels over ten business days supports an
unreachable note.

### 4.2 Inspection

The covered auto is inspected before repair or disposal where the policy
reserves that right. Where the vehicle has already been repaired, the file
records why inspection was not possible and what documentation was accepted in
its place.

### 4.3 Documentation Retained

| Document | Retained | Retention period |
|---|---|---|
| Appraisal estimate and supplements | Always | Current year plus two |
| Valuation report | Total loss only | Current year plus two |
| Rental agreement | Loss of use only | Current year plus two |
| Police report | Theft, vandalism, unidentified vehicle | Current year plus two |
| Written communications to claimant | Always | Current year plus two |

## 5. Loss of Use

### 5.1 Trigger

Loss of use is considered once the covered auto has been withdrawn from use for
longer than the period the governing form states. The adjuster reads the period
from the form; it is not assumed.

### 5.2 Rate and Duration

The per-day amount and the maximum number of days come from the governing form
and, where one is attached, from the endorsement that amends it. Where a form
and an attached endorsement state different amounts, the endorsement governs the
provision it names and the file note records both figures and which was applied.

Where the endorsement's status is unconfirmed, no figure is applied and the file
is referred. An adjuster does not choose between two documents.

### 5.3 Comparable Class

| Covered auto class | Authorised rental class | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Economy or compact | Same or lower | Adjuster |
| Midsize or full size | Same or lower | Adjuster |
| Light truck or SUV | Same or lower | Adjuster |
| Any, upgraded class | One class higher | Supervisor |

## 6. Settlement

### 6.1 Repairable Losses

The settlement is the lesser of the cost to repair with parts of like kind and
quality and the actual cash value, less the deductible shown on the
declarations. The deductible applied is the one on the declarations, not the
standard schedule in the form.

### 6.2 Total Losses

The offer is communicated in writing and states the valuation basis, the
components included, the deductible applied, and any right to reopen that the
rated state requires. Components required by the rated state are included
whether or not the form names them.

| Step | Standard | Measured from |
|---|---|---|
| Valuation obtained | 10 business days | Total loss declared |
| Written offer | 5 business days | Valuation obtained |
| Payment after acceptance | 10 business days | Acceptance |
| Denial communicated in writing | 5 business days | Position taken |

### 6.3 Labelling Payments

Each payment states the coverage under which it is made. A payment covering more
than one coverage is issued as separate items or itemised on the draft advice.

## 7. Denial and Partial Denial

A denial in whole or in part is communicated in writing, references the specific
policy provision, condition or exclusion relied on, and is copied to the claim
file. A ground not stated in the denial is not available later. Where the rated
state requires the factual and legal basis for each reason, both are given.

## 8. Escalation

Refer to the claims desk supervisor where:

- the governing form or endorsement status is unconfirmed;
- two governing documents state different figures for the same provision and
  nothing on the declarations settles which applies;
- a prior determination on the same policy reached a different result;
- the claimant asserts a coverage the declarations does not show;
- the answer requires a judgment the documents do not contain.

Escalation is recorded with the reason and the owner it was referred to.
