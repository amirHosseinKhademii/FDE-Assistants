## 1. How to Use This Manual

This manual covers eligibility, classification and tier placement for the
personal auto program. Anything it does not address is referred before binding.
It governs whether a policy may be written and on what terms at issue. It does
not govern what an issued policy pays; once a policy is issued, the form, the
endorsements and the declarations determine coverage, and the remedies available
under this manual run through cancellation, non-renewal or rescission rather
than through the claim.

Where a state manual exists for a jurisdiction, the state manual governs for
risks in that jurisdiction and this manual applies only where the state manual
is silent.

## 2. Producer Authority

### 2.1 New Business Binding

A producer may bind a risk that meets every eligibility rule in Part 3 and falls
in an acceptable class under Part 4, within the limits of the table below.
Binding authority is granted only in accordance with the rules and procedures
set out in this manual.

| Coverage | Bindable without referral | Referral required above |
|---|---|---|
| Bodily Injury Liability | Up to program maximum | Program maximum |
| Property Damage Liability | Up to program maximum | Program maximum |
| Collision | Any offered deductible | Vehicle value threshold |
| Comprehensive | Any offered deductible | Vehicle value threshold |
| Rental Reimbursement | Any offered schedule | None |

### 2.2 Endorsement Binding

A mid-term change is processed within five business days of the request. A
change that would move the risk into an unacceptable class under Part 3 is not
bound and is referred.

### 2.3 Suspension of Binding Authority

Binding authority is suspended where a producer's bound book shows a
referral-rule miss rate above the program threshold over a rolling quarter, and
is restored on completion of program retraining.

## 3. Eligibility

### 3.1 Acceptable Risks

A risk is acceptable where the named insured holds a valid licence in the rated
state, every listed operator is disclosed, and every vehicle is registered to a
listed operator or to the named insured.

### 3.2 Unacceptable Vehicles

| Category | Test | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Salvage or rebuilt title | Title brand | Decline |
| Gross vehicle weight above program limit | Registration | Decline |
| Vehicle held for hire | Disclosed use | Refer |
| Vehicle used for delivery of goods | Disclosed use | Refer |
| Vehicle used to carry passengers for a fee | Disclosed use | Refer |
| Antique or collector | Registration class | Refer |

A disclosed use that falls in a refer row is referred at application and the
disposition is recorded. A refer disposition is not a decline and is not
communicated to the applicant as one.

### 3.3 Unacceptable Drivers

| Category | Lookback (months) | Threshold | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major violation | 60 | One | Decline |
| At-fault losses | 36 | Three or more | Decline |
| At-fault losses | 36 | Two | Refer |
| Licence suspension | 36 | One | Refer |
| Unlicensed operator in household | — | Any | Refer |

### 3.4 Excluded Drivers

A household member may be excluded only on a signed exclusion form retained in
the underwriting file. An exclusion that is not on file is not effective, and
the operator is rated.

## 4. Classification and Tier Placement

### 4.1 Driver Classification

| Class code | Age band | Marital status | Licence status | Years experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Under 21 | Any | Valid | Under 3 |
| A2 | 21 to 24 | Any | Valid | 3 or more |
| B1 | 25 to 64 | Single | Valid | 3 or more |
| B2 | 25 to 64 | Married | Valid | 3 or more |
| C1 | 65 and over | Any | Valid | 3 or more |

### 4.2 Tier Assignment

| Tier | Qualifying characteristics | Movement at renewal |
|---|---|---|
| Preferred | Five years claim-free, qualifying credit tier | Held unless a surcharge trigger occurs |
| Standard | No major violation, fewer than two at-fault losses | Moves to Preferred after five clean years |
| Essential | Does not qualify for Standard | Moves to Standard after three clean years |

Tier placement is made from the filed matrix. A placement that does not come
from the filed matrix is a deviation, and a deviation is itself a violation
whether or not the resulting premium is lower.

### 4.3 Licence State Matrix

| Licence state or status | Rating classification |
|---|---|
| Rated state, valid | Standard classification |
| Other state, valid | Standard classification |
| International permit | Refer |
| Permit only | Refer |

## 7. Coverages and Limits Offered

| Coverage | Available limits | Deductible options | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bodily Injury Liability | Program schedule by tier | None | State minimum is a floor |
| Property Damage Liability | Program schedule by tier | None | State minimum is a floor |
| Collision | Actual cash value | Program schedule | — |
| Comprehensive | Actual cash value | Program schedule | — |
| Rental Reimbursement | Program schedule by tier | None | Optional on Essential |

Where a state's mandated minimum exceeds the limit shown in the program
schedule, the mandated minimum applies and the schedule is read up to it.

## 8. Policy Administration

### 8.1 Cancellation

Cancellation for a reason other than non-payment is made only on a ground the
rated state permits, with the notice period that state requires.

### 8.2 Non-renewal

Non-renewal follows the notice period of the rated state and states the reason
where that state requires one.

### 8.3 Reinstatement

A policy cancelled for non-payment may be reinstated within the program window
without a lapse surcharge where no loss occurred during the lapse.

### 8.4 Rewrite

A rewrite is treated as new business for tier placement and carries the loss
history of the prior policy.

## 9. Material Misrepresentation

An undisclosed operator or vehicle discovered after issue is referred to
underwriting. The available remedies are those the policy and the rated state
permit, and they are exercised through the policy rather than through a claim
decision.
