## Summary of Determination

Rental reimbursement was paid at the schedule stated in PA-2022-04 section 4.4
for the period the covered auto was withdrawn from use. No endorsement amending
that section appeared on the declarations in force on the date of loss.

## Loss Facts

The covered auto sustained collision damage on 2023-04-18 and was withdrawn from
use on 2023-04-19. Repairs were completed and the vehicle returned to use on
2023-05-01, a period of twelve days.

## Documents Relied On

| Document | Section | What it established |
|---|---|---|
| AUT-4487 declarations | Coverages In Force | Rental reimbursement selected |
| AUT-4487 declarations | Endorsements Attached | None attached |
| PA-2022-04 | 4.3 Schedule | Per-day amount and maximum days |
| PA-2022-04 | 4.4 Rental Reimbursement | Trigger and duration rule |

## Reasoning

The declarations named PA-2022-04 as the form and listed no endorsements, so
section 4.4 of that form governed without amendment. The 24-hour withdrawal
threshold in 4.4 was met. The twelve-day period fell inside the per-occurrence
maximum, so the maximum was not reached and no proration applied.

## Notes on Later Use

This determination construed section 4.4 of PA-2022-04. It is authority for a
later claim only where the policy in force on that later date of loss uses the
same wording in that section. Where a later edition changed the wording, the
later edition's text governs and this determination is history, not authority.
