# BUL-2024-07 — Rental Reimbursement: Documentation and Continuation After Total Loss

> practice engagement. Do not use for anything real.

> Supersedes: BUL-2022-03 · Status: Current
> Applies to: PA-2023-01, PA-2023-01-TX, PA-2023-01-CA, PA-2023-01-NY, PA-2023-01-FL

## 1. Purpose

This bulletin replaces Section 6.2 of the Claims Handling Procedures (PRC-006,
revision 4) on the documentation an adjuster must obtain before authorising
rental reimbursement, and on how long reimbursement continues once a vehicle is
declared a total loss. It does not change what any policy form pays. Coverage is
determined by the policy, its endorsements and the declarations.

## 2. Effect on Prior Guidance

This bulletin supersedes BUL-2022-03 in full. BUL-2022-03 remains in the archive
for claims with a date of loss before 2024-07-15 and must not be applied to
later losses.

## 3. Documentation Required Before Authorisation

| Item | Required | Obtained from | Retain in file |
|------|----------|---------------|----------------|
| Rental agreement | Always | Claimant or rental vendor | Yes |
| Daily rate and class | Always | Rental agreement | Yes |
| Repair facility estimate | Where repairable | Assigned appraiser | Yes |
| Total loss determination | Where declared | Appraisal unit | Yes |

An adjuster may not authorise a rental class above the class of the covered
auto. Where the claimant has already rented a higher class, authorise at the
comparable class and record the difference as claimant responsibility in the
file note.

## 4. Continuation After a Total Loss Declaration

Reimbursement continues from the date the loss is reported until the earliest
of: the day the vehicle is returned to use; the day the actual cash value offer
is communicated in writing, plus the continuation period in the table below; or
the day the per-occurrence maximum on the governing form is reached.

| Circumstance | Continuation after written offer | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Offer accepted | 3 calendar days | This bulletin |
| Offer disputed, appraisal not demanded | 5 calendar days | This bulletin |
| Appraisal demanded under the policy | Until appraisal award | Policy form |

The per-occurrence maximum on the governing form is a ceiling that this bulletin
cannot raise. Where the continuation period in the table would exceed it, the
form controls and the file note must say so.

## 5. Authority Limits

| Action | Adjuster | Senior adjuster | Supervisor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authorise within form maximum | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Extend beyond continuation table | No | Yes | Yes |
| Authorise above comparable class | No | No | Yes |

## 6. Escalation

Refer to the claims desk supervisor where the governing form is unconfirmed,
where two documents state different per-day amounts, or where the claimant
asserts an endorsement that does not appear on the declarations.

## 7. Questions

Direct questions to Claims Practices.
